## 1. What is Business Intelligence?

Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analysing, and transforming raw organisational data into meaningful information that supports strategic and operational decision-making.  

BI combines data warehousing, analytical processing, reporting, and data visualisation tools to help leaders:

- Understand past performance  
- Monitor current operations  
- Anticipate future outcomes  

In practice, BI systems integrate data from multiple sources into a unified analytical environment, enabling organisations to identify trends, measure KPIs, detect anomalies, and align daily activities with long-term goals.

---

## 2. Why Organisations Use Dashboards

Organisations adopt interactive dashboards because they convert complex datasets into visual, accessible formats. Key reasons include:

- **Speed of insight** — Critical metrics appear in seconds instead of hours of manual reporting.
- **Self-service analytics** — Managers can filter and explore data independently  
- **Single source of truth** — Metrics from multiple systems are consolidated into one trusted view. 
- **Accountability & transparency** — Shared dashboards align teams around common goals  
- **Early warning capability** — Visual alerts help leadership detect issues before they escalate  

---

## 3. How Dashboards Improve Decision-Making

Interactive dashboards improve decision-making by turning static reports into dynamic analytical tools. Decision-makers can:

- Filter and drill down from high-level KPIs into detailed transactions  
- Compare performance across time periods, segments, and geographies  
- Identify patterns and anomalies through visual cues  
- Align strategy with evidence rather than anecdotal observations  

Dashboards reduce the time between observation and action, enabling leaders to respond faster and with greater confidence.

## 4. Business Objective of the Selected Dataset

The selected dataset is the **Global Superstore Dataset**. It contains approximately 51,000 order-level transaction records spanning 2011–2014 across 147 countries and seven global markets (US, APAC, EU, LATAM, Africa, EMEA, and Canada).

### Business Objective
Design an interactive executive dashboard that enables senior management to:

- Monitor overall sales performance and profitability  
- Identify which regions and markets generate the highest sales and profit  
- Understand which customer segments contribute the most revenue  
- Determine which product categories and products are most profitable  
- Observe sales and profit trends over time  
- Formulate data-driven recommendations to improve business performance  

By transforming this global transactional dataset into an interactive Power BI dashboard, management gains the ability to move from raw data to strategic action.
